## 💡 Coding Tip of the Day
**Refine System Prompts Over Step-by-Step Instructions for Agents:**
As agentic workflows become standard, focus on refining your *system prompts and boundary conditions* rather than step-by-step instructions. Let the autonomous agents figure out the "how" while you strictly define the "what" and safety constraints.
