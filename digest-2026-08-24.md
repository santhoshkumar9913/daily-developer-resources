## 💡 Coding Tip of the Day

**Refining Agentic System Prompts**
As agentic workflows become the standard, shift your prompt engineering strategy. Instead of providing rigid, step-by-step instructions, focus on defining strict boundary conditions, state constraints, and output schemas (e.g., using JSON Schema or XML tags). Let the autonomous agent figure out the "how" while you strictly enforce the "what" and the safety constraints.
