## 💡 Coding Tip of the Day
**Clean up your commit history with `git rebase -i`!**
Before pushing your feature branch, use `git rebase -i HEAD~N` (where N is the number of commits) to interactively squash, edit, or reword your commits. It keeps the main branch history clean and understandable for your team.
