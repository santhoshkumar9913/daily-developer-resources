## 💡 Coding Tip of the Day
**Optimize Prompt Caching:** When interacting with LLMs via API, always place your system instructions and static context at the very beginning of your context window. Keeping the prefix consistent maximizes prompt caching hits, which can drastically reduce inference costs (saving you from massive billing surprises) and significantly decrease latency on subsequent requests.
