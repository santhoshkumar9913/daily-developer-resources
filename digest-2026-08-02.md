## 💡 Coding Tip of the Day
**Optimize Your Prompt Caching:**
When building LLM integrations, place your static context, system prompts, and large reference documents at the *very top* of the payload, and append the dynamic user inputs at the *bottom*. This structure maximizes prompt cache hit rates across providers (like AWS Bedrock or Anthropic API), significantly reducing latency and preventing unexpected massive billing spikes due to cache misses.
