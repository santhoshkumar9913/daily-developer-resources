## 💡 Coding Tip of the Day
**Managing Agentic AI Workflows:** 
As autonomous agents become standard, focus on refining your *system prompts and boundary conditions* rather than step-by-step instructions. Let the agents figure out the "how" while you strictly define the "what" and the safety constraints. Set rigid cost and token guardrails to avoid unexpected API bills from looping agents!
