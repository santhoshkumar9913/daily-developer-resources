## 💡 Coding Tip of the Day
**Optimize Your Prompts for AI Agents**
As agentic workflows become the new standard, focus on refining your *system prompts and boundary conditions* rather than writing strict, step-by-step instructions. Let the agents figure out the "how" while you explicitly define the "what" and the safety constraints. This reduces long-term maintenance costs and leverages the AI's ability to self-correct and plan dynamically.
