## 💡 Coding Tip of the Day
**Efficient Multi-Agent Architecture:**
When building agentic workflows, always implement a "Supervisor" or "Router" pattern. Instead of every agent talking to every other agent, have a central supervisor that delegates tasks and aggregates results. This drastically reduces token usage, speeds up execution time, and makes debugging much simpler.
