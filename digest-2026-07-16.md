## 💡 Daily Coding Tip
**Optimize Prompt Caching for Cost-Efficiency:**
When working with Large Language Models in production, structure your prompts to maximize caching. Always place static context, system instructions, and large reference documents at the *beginning* of the prompt, and append dynamic or frequently changing user queries at the *end*. A single cache miss on a massive prompt can lead to dramatic spikes in your API bill, so monitor your cache hit rates closely!
