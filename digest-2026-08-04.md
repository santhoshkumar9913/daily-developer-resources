## 💡 Coding Tip of the Day
**Refining Agentic Workflows:** As autonomous agents become the standard, shift your engineering focus toward refining *system prompts and boundary conditions* rather than writing strict, step-by-step instructions. Define the "what" and the safety constraints firmly, and allow the model to figure out the "how".
