## 💡 Daily Coding Tip: Maximize LLM Prompt Caching
When building applications that send large amounts of context (like whole codebases or extensive documentation) to an LLM API, **utilize prompt caching** to save on costs and reduce latency. 
*How to do it:* Structure your prompts so that the large, static context is placed at the very beginning of the prompt, and place your dynamic, per-request user instructions at the end. This maximizes the chance of a cache hit on the provider's side!
