## 💡 Coding Tip of the Day
**Implement Strict Cost Guardrails for LLMs:** 
A single prompt caching miss or runaway agent loop can cost thousands of dollars in a production environment. Always implement strict spending limits, alerts, and caching checks *before* deploying an LLM feature to scale. When working with agentic workflows, focus on refining your system prompts and boundary conditions rather than step-by-step instructions—let the agents figure out the "how" while you strictly define the "what" and the safety constraints.
