## 💡 Coding Tip of the Day
**Define Boundaries, Not Steps for AI Agents**
As agentic workflows become standard, focus on refining your *system prompts and boundary conditions* rather than providing step-by-step instructions. Let the agents figure out the "how" while you strictly define the "what" and the safety constraints. This reduces maintenance and improves the agent's ability to handle edge cases.
