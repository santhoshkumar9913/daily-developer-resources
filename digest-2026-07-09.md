# Daily Developer Digest - 2026-07-09

## 🤖 AI News Highlights
Based on the latest industry updates:
* **Agentic Workflows Go Mainstream:** We are seeing a massive industry shift from conversational chatbots to autonomous "agentic" systems. Enterprises are actively replacing traditional apps with background agents that plan and execute multi-step workflows.
* **Hyper-Efficient Edge Models:** Highly compressed, privacy-first Small Language Models (SLMs) are running locally on zero-latency devices, bypassing the cloud entirely and drastically reducing inference costs.
* **Policy & Society:** California's Governor has signed a first-of-its-kind executive order preparing for AI economic disruptions, and the Vatican is set to release a dedicated encyclical on AI ethics.
* **Data Center Pushback:** Rapid AI expansion is causing local friction, with citizens in places like Utah pushing back against the environmental and energy footprint of massive new AI data centers.

## 💡 Coding Tip of the Day
**Refine Boundaries, Not Steps:** As agentic workflows become standard, focus on refining your *system prompts and boundary conditions* rather than step-by-step instructions. Let the agents figure out the "how" while you strictly define the "what" and the safety constraints.
