## 💡 Daily Coding Tip
**Optimize LLM Prompts with Few-Shot Examples**
When building AI-integrated apps, providing 2-3 concrete examples (few-shot prompting) is often more reliable than complex zero-shot instructions. It anchors the model's output format, drastically reducing parsing errors and minimizing prompt drift over time!
