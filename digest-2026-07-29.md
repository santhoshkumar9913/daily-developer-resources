## 💡 Daily Coding Tip
**Implement Prompt Caching Guardrails**
When utilizing foundational LLMs in production, unexpected prompt caching misses can lead to massive API bills. Always implement strict cost guardrails, budget caps, and thorough observability (using telemetry tools) to monitor cache hit rates and safely abort runaway loops in agentic architectures!
