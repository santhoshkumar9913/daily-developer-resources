## 💡 Coding Tip of the Day
**Optimize Prompt Caching to Avoid Bill Shocks!**
When building LLM integrations, a single prompt caching miss in a high-volume loop can cost you thousands of dollars. Implement strict cost guardrails, use exact prefix matching for your cached contexts, and always set budget alerts in your cloud provider before deploying autonomous agents to production.
