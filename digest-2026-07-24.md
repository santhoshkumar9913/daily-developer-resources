## 💡 Coding Tip of the Day
**Refining Agentic Workflows**
As agentic workflows become standard, focus on refining your *system prompts and boundary conditions* rather than step-by-step instructions. Let the agents figure out the "how" while you strictly define the "what" and the safety constraints. Use explicit schema validation to ensure the outputs match your pipeline's expectations.
